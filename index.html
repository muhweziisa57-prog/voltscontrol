What We Do

At Volt Controls, we design and build heavy-duty **Industrial Electrical Control Panels**, **Automatic Changeover Switches (ATS)**, and tailored automation systems. We assemble precision power distributions using top-grade contactors, timers, and relays to keep machinery running smoothly.

How It Works

Our smart systems remove the human error from power management. When the main grid cuts out, our controllers instantly detect the failure, safely ignite backup generators, and seamlessly swap over the loads without blowing components or risking electrical accidents.

Where It's Applied

Our systems protect critical setups across East Africa—operating in **industrial manufacturing factories**, commercial office hubs, residential apartments, large-scale milling workshops, and agricultural pump systems where uninterrupted power is non-negotiable.
